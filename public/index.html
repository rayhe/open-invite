How it works

1. Create an event and pick a theme.
2. Add guests manually or import a CSV.
3. Hit Send invitations — each guest gets a personal RSVP link by email.
4. Watch RSVPs roll in on your dashboard. Nudge the stragglers.

Runs on your Firebase project and your Resend account. The demo key scope needed is send-only.
